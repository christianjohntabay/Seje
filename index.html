About My Internship

Built in silence. Proven through results.

During my internship at DepEd – Schools Division of Zamboanga del Sur Information Office, I was assigned to different technical and administrative tasks that strengthened my skills in IT support, networking, automation, and problem-solving.

I performed preventive maintenance on employees' computers, installed CCTV systems, created internet cables using UTP and RJ45 connectors, and helped speed up documentation tasks by assisting in the creation of DAT files.

One of my biggest contributions was developing a Python-based automation tool using Streamlit, Pandas, Matplotlib, and OpenPyXL that generated answer distributions, charts, and Excel reports automatically. This significantly reduced manual work and helped improve efficiency in office tasks.

Reflection

My internship journey became one of the most important experiences in my growth as an IT student. Throughout the training, I learned how technology can improve workflow, productivity, and communication inside an organization.

I experienced real-world challenges that strengthened my technical skills, critical thinking, teamwork, and adaptability. From troubleshooting computers to creating automation tools using Python, I discovered how important innovation and initiative are in the workplace.

This internship also taught me professionalism, responsibility, patience, and confidence. It inspired me to continue improving my skills in programming, networking, and system development to become a better IT professional in the future.
