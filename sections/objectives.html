OBJECTIVES

GENERAL OBJECTIVES:

In response to meet the industry’s demand for technically competent and well-rounded IT graduates, the practicum/OJT program aims to:

  1. Provide the students an opportunity to be exposed in an actual work setting and assimilate their academics into practical applications.
  2. Develop and instill among the students the industry-desired values of a positive work attitude, good human relations, competency in technical skills pertaining to their fields of study, and the openness to keep learning and reinvent themselves.
  3. Enable the student to gain experience and relate and apply the theories and concepts they learned in school/classrooms to real industry scenarios and problems.
  4. Instill the importance of human relations in the student in the working environment.
